    <h1 text-align="center">Buffalo Chicken Salad</h1>
    <img class="recipePic" src="{{ page.image }}" alt="Buffalo Chicken Salad">
    <div>
      <h4>Ingredients</h4>
      <ul>
        <li>1 lb Chicken Tenderloins (or breasts)</li>
        <li>2/3 Cups of Mayonnaise (Preferrably Duke's)</li>
        <li>6 tbsps of Buffalo Sauce</li>
        <li>Celery Salt</li>
        <li>Garlic Powder</li>
        <li>Salt</li>
        <li>Onion Powder</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h4>Recipe</h4>
        <ol>
          <li>Place chicken in a pan with enough water/chicken broth to cover</li>
          <li>Optional: Add seasoning to the water</li>
          <li>Bring the water to a boil and then immediately cool to a simmer for 8-10 minutes</li>
          <li>Recommended internal temp is 165 F</li>
          <li>Remove chicken from water and shred using a food processor or preferred method</li>
          <li>Mix in all other ingredients to taste</li>
        </ol>
    </div>
    <div>
      <p>All together, cook time is about 20-30 minutes</p>
    </div>
    <div>
      <h4>About</h4>
      <p>This is a copycat recipe from Chicken Salad Chick. It was so good there that I had to find a recipe and make it for myself.</p>
      <h4>Goes Well With</h4>
      <p>Best with club crackers or on bread as a sandwich. But really it's so good on it's own that it can be eaten straight from the bowl.</p>
